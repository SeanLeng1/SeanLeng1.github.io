PhD in Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University

Jixuan Leng

Research on efficient, reliable, and aligned foundation models.

I am a first-year PhD in Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University # advised by # Prof. William W. Cohen. Before CMU, I completed my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Rochester, where I was advised by Prof. Jiebo Luo.

My research focuses on LLMs post-training. I am currently a Student Researcher at Google Research advised by Dr. Si Si, and I also collaborate with Prof. Jiaxin Huang and Prof. Haohan Wang.
